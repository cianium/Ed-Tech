# ADR-004 — Conditional authentication provider for Iran
**Status:** Open (release-blocking for M1).

Docs 06–07 use Supabase Auth assumptions; the later Blueprint explicitly makes any provider conditional for Iran operations. We do not claim Supabase, email providers, hosting providers or AI endpoints are reachable, legally eligible, private or inexpensive. M0 builds a provider-neutral app foundation and avoids provider-coupled identity tables. Prior to M1: verify service eligibility, ToS, sanctions/jurisdiction concerns, data processing, child eligibility, operational access, email/password recovery, backups, costs and failover. Record findings with dates and official terms; seek founder approval for concrete provider.
